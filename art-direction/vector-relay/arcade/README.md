# Cold Signal — arcade motion revision

The creator selected **Cold Front's palette with Black Signal's everything else**, and rejected the prior motion examples as dull. This revision fixes that combination and replaces the poster-first presentation with a moving arcade sequence.

Open [the arcade preview](http://localhost:5186/vector-relay/arcade/). The original [Vector Relay entry](http://localhost:5186/vector-relay/) now leads here. The [earlier poster studies](../poster-studies.html) remain available for comparison.

## The accepted visual combination

All eight palette channels come directly from Cold Front: navy field, cyan signal, violet accent, peach danger, pale information, and the original supporting tones. Black Signal supplies the forked pilot contour, circular rescue marker, threat motifs, radial wireframes, segmented instruments, and monospaced supporting type. Prismatic sheets and the other treatments' type/masking systems are excluded from this revision.

The art-direction skill is applied as a refinement of the creator's stated choice. No new taste interview or approval gate is needed.

## What moves now

- Four pilots orbit quickly, change depth, and leave short directional trails.
- Continuous inward shots have bright cores and longer fading tails.
- Enemy formations travel out from the center; staged shots arrive at staged hit points.
- Hits eject angular cyan/violet/peach fragments, short rings, and score marks.
- Chain bursts have a brief hit stop, an expanding shockwave, and optional bounded camera punch.
- Gap walls cross the rails while the depth field and range marks rush outward.
- Rescue rush brings a pilot to a downed partner and finishes with the partner back in action.

**Crossfire** presents sustained four-pilot action. **Overdrive** increases movement speed, target density, fire cadence, and chain-burst frequency. **Rescue rush** demonstrates an exposed rescue inside the action. These are three action presets for one visual identity.

Click **Shockwave** or press **Space** while the canvas/page is focused to clear a group of visible targets and sample the impact. The playback controls pause, replay, and scrub the sequence. Impact camera toggles the bounded shake/zoom separately from the core action. A browser reduced-motion preference disables impact camera on entry; the creator's requested action preview still moves.

**Sound** is off initially. Its optional original procedural beat, bass, hit chirps, and pulse are generated locally with Web Audio. Pause or a hidden tab suspends audio. **Save 8s clip** exports a silent recording of the canvas, preferring native MP4 when supported and WebM otherwise; nothing is uploaded.

## Scope and evidence

This is **scripted motion choreography**, not a live multiplayer match or gameplay balance test. It uses the existing perspective helpers and original glyph drawing, with minimum on-screen symbol sizes for this preview. Enemy kills, timing, score, rescue, walls, and camera beats are authored for evaluation. The playable game's rules, renderer, server, and tuning are untouched. No claim of human enjoyment or recognition follows from these visuals.

The former studies and their verification describe their historical revision. Current checks, source hashes, screenshots, and the recorded clip are in [evidence](evidence/verification.json). The exact palette match, Black Signal type/grammar, rescue transition, manual pulse behavior, finite draw geometry, and alpha/state preservation were checked across 420 sampled frames; browser playback and export were checked separately.
