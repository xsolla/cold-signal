RELAY · FIRST MARKER

RELAY · REVISED MARKER

Open circular bracket + two separated bars; same bounds, progress, and position.

Marks are drawn at the game's 26px outer-rail size, then at its middle and inner depth scales, using a 690px scene. The 23px progress radius is separate from the body. This is a visual hypothesis; human recognition is untested.
