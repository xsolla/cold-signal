# Art-direction v1.1.0 — Delve Together evaluation

**Method:** Self-reviewed real-project application by the same agent that produced the artifacts. This is neither an independent evaluation nor a human playtest. The skill source was frozen before use and left unchanged. No model-scored grade or fabricated interview result is attached.

**Trial scope:** Apply art-direction to Delve Together with three visual systems within its established stark arcade promise. Preserve the three-depth tunnel, game rules, positions, HUD information, and rescue decision. Compare concept boards and the same crowded gameplay moment, inspect small sizes and grayscale, then make one bounded correction. Keep all alternatives as proposals. The user confirmed this breadth and supplied Marathon and iQOO motion references during the trial; the final proposals incorporate that steering.

## Findings from this application

| Skill behavior | Observed result | Practical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reuse existing taste input and ask only missing questions | Two short questions confirmed three stark arcade looks and supplied Marathon/iQOO; actual reference pages and sampled motion were inspected | User approved the scope and references, not the resulting candidates |
| Separate product constraints from visual freedom | All candidates use one constructed state, exact projection, shared HUD positions/copy, and distinct native shape grammars | A constructed still does not show movement, timing, or difficulty |
| Compare independent concept systems | Forked instrument craft, heavy salvage clamps, and ivory/ink swept wave forms have separately authored environment/object/UI and motion samples | Their distinction is the producing agent's visual assessment; a blind human review is still useful. Concept and context code were authored together, so the separate early-production gate was not exercised |
| Diagnose at relevant size | Full scene, 320×200 contexts, and un-enlarged inner-rail glyphs reveal that the original perspective makes small objects extremely small | Readability may require a separate gameplay/presentation decision beyond art treatment |
| Keep corrections bounded | Relay's diamond rescue body was changed inside the same envelope; 50% progress radius and positions stay fixed | The revised marker's paired bars may suggest pause; creator recognition should test this |
| Keep evidence honest and scope contained | Native Canvas proposals, frozen baseline, source hashes, explicit constructed state, blank review form, reference-role records | No raster generation, production export, game integration, or human recognition evidence |

## What worked

The fixed-versus-free distinction is useful for this project. It allows visibly different craft and environmental systems while protecting the tunnel geometry and exposed rescue. The early board requirement makes the differences inspectable before a complete playable reskin. The refinement instructions provide a concrete constraint: improve a rescue marker without quietly increasing its scale or altering other systems.

The skill also fits a procedural Canvas game without forcing generated raster artwork. That is a useful portability result: actual native artifacts can expose both style and engineering constraints.

## Friction and suggestions for a future skill revision

1. **Discovery versus use:** The skill was available in the PR, not in the requested base branch or the current session's installed-skill catalog. It had to be copied explicitly and read. This trial validates applying its instructions after loading them, not automatic discovery or implicit invocation.
2. **The handoff from intake to action remains interpretive.** Provisional boards were authored while optional intake was pending, then revised after the creator supplied references. That supported progress, but should not be confused with following an explicitly requested interview-first gate. The final brief records real answers separately from the agent's design interpretation.
3. **Dynamic games need a motion calibration follow-up.** The added abstract motion studies respond to the iQOO reference, but they do not test enemy movement, wall expansion, visual effects, or four-player clutter. A short identical replay segment would be a stronger next step after taste selection.
4. **The legibility bottleneck may exceed the art scope.** At the preserved projection, a nominal inner-rail body is 6.96px before further thumbnail reduction. Nearby P1/P2 labels overlap in the constructed rescue, including the original renderer. A new outline cannot guarantee recognition there. The next decision may concern minimum symbol size or contextual labels, which should be explicitly authorized and tested separately.

The reusable skill was not modified to make the evaluation look better. These are candidate feedback items, not automatically accepted defects or universal prescriptions.

## Verdict and next use

The skill produced a concrete, reviewable exploration and a narrow revision on a real project while preserving the current game. Recommend **Vector Relay for the next calibration**, with user taste still open. The trial supports usefulness for structured native-art exploration; it does not establish superiority over an unskilled baseline, player recognition, enjoyment, or production readiness.
